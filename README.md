# Judes-Monisha

Dashboard Purpose
The primary purpose of this dashboard is to provide hospital management with a single interactive view of claim submissions and financial performance, enabling faster identification of high-value areas, insurance trends, doctor-level performance, and departmental activity.

Report: Hospital Submission Report
Period: November 2025
Dashboard Type: Hospital Claims & Submission Analytics

Key Objectives
Monitor total submitted claim amounts.
Analyze procedure, laboratory, and consultation costs.
Compare submitted amounts across insurance providers.
Evaluate doctor-wise claim performance.
Track average patient/claim value per doctor.
Analyze claim distribution by insurance.
Review claim costs by category.
Filter results by hospital department.

 Dashboard Components
1. Doctor Summary Table
Provides doctor-level information including:
Doctor name
Total visits
Total submitted amount
A total row summarizes the overall number of visits and submitted amount.

2. Submitted Amount by Insurance
Visualizes the total submitted claim amount by insurance provider.

3. Patient Average per Doctor
A gauge visualization showing the average patient/claim amount per doctor.

Current average: approximately AED 6.82K.

4. Claim Count by Insurance
A pie chart showing the distribution of claims between insurance providers.
This helps identify which insurance providers contribute the largest share of claim activity.

5. Doctor-wise Performance
A bar chart compares doctors based on their total submitted claim amounts.

This allows users to quickly identify:
Highest-value doctors
Lower submission volumes
Differences in claim contribution across doctors

6. Claim Cost Summary
A donut chart breaks down claim costs into categories such as:
Consultation
Laboratory
Deductible
Net amount
Submitted amount
This provides a high-level view of how the overall claim value is distributed.

7. Department Filter
The dashboard includes an interactive department slicer with departments such as:

General Practitioner
Immunology
Medical Department
Obstetrics and Gynecology
Occupational Therapy
Orthopedic Surgery
Pediatric Dentistry
Physiotherapist
Physiotherapy
Rehabilitation
Speech Therapist
Speech Therapist / Speech & Language
Users can select a department to dynamically filter the dashboard.

Reporting Period
November 2025

The dashboard can be extended to support additional monthly reporting periods and historical comparisons.
